Scope: the Journa app shell and its screens (Today, Notes, Tasks, Lists, Search, Settings, entry editor, Trash). Mode: Operate.

Audience/job: privacy-minded people capturing quickly on a phone (installed PWA) and writing longer entries at night. English, Tamil, Hindi.

Constraints: offline, no third-party requests, system fonts, WCAG 2.2 AA, phone-first, light and dark.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each day is drawn anew, like a kolam at the threshold: writing joins the day's dots into one continuous line. Refuses the grey-list-plus-blue-accent notes app.

OWN-WORLD: Kaavi red-oxide shell (top bar, kolam band, primary actions) over a cool limewash ground; rice-flour white line work; turmeric marks today and selection; leaf green confirms. Dark: wet-earth ground, kaavi deepened, rice lines. System sans; rank by weight and case on a tight 1.125 scale. Standard web controls throughout.

STORY: The visitor opens to Today, sees at a glance which days this month they wrote, writes today's entry, ticks tasks, and trusts the backup chip.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Kaavi top bar (Journa, backup status chip, "Today" and date); kolam band with the month's pulli grid, written days looped and consecutive days joined, today's dot in turmeric; then journal card, today's tasks, lists; compose button bottom-right above a five-tab bottom bar (desktop: tabs become a left rail).

FORM: Kolam, position 7 of my ordered list; signature move = month streak drawn as a kolam line, echoed as checklist progress. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
